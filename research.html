Research overview

Intervention research that is practical, collaborative, and family-centered.

Dr. Kunze's work focuses on helping young children with developmental differences by developing supports that are accessible, culturally responsive, and grounded in real family routines. Her projects often combine telehealth, caregiver coaching, and naturalistic ABA to improve child and family outcomes.

Her research spans autism, traumatic brain injury, military families, caregiver coaching, and early childhood intervention systems.

Research philosophy

Accessible science with real-world value

Across her current projects, Dr. Kunze focuses on intervention models that fit the people and systems they are meant to serve. That means studying what happens when support is delivered through telehealth, when family goals guide intervention, and when cultural and linguistic adaptation is built into the work from the start.
