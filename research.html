Research

Her work centers family-focused intervention, telehealth coaching, autism support, and traumatic brain injury systems.

Theme 1

Family interventions

Helping caregivers build confidence through practical, values-based coaching and naturalistic ABA support.

Theme 2

Early intervention and autism

Research on diagnosis, parent-mediated support, telehealth delivery, and young children with developmental differences.

Theme 3

Traumatic brain injury

Projects that build school, family, and community systems of support for children and youth with TBI.
