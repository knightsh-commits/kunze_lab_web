Behavior analysis · early intervention · family-centered research

Supporting children. Empowering families. Advancing early intervention.

Dr. Megan Kunze is an Assistant Research Professor at the University of Oregon whose work focuses on family interventions, early intervention, autism, traumatic brain injury, and naturalistic applied behavior analysis. Her career bridges research, teaching, clinical practice, and community partnership.
