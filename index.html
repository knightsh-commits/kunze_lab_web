About Dr. Kunze

Welcome to the official research portal for Dr. Megan Kunze, Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Special Education and Clinical Sciences at the University of Oregon.


This lab focuses on improving outcomes for individuals with disabilities, developing behavioral interventions, and supporting families and educators through evidence-based practices.
