Research that supports children and families.

Dr. Megan Kunze is an Assistant Research Professor at the University of Oregon whose work focuses on family interventions, supporting young children with autism, traumatic brain injury, and other developmental differences, and building practical systems of care through naturalistic applied behavior analysis.
