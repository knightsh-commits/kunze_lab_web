About Dr. Megan Kunze

Professional biography, academic focus, and the values that guide her work.

Bio

Family-centered research and practice

Dr. Megan Kunze is an Assistant Research Professor and Associate Director of the Center on Brain Injury Research and Training at the University of Oregon. Her research focuses on family interventions and on supporting young children with autism, traumatic brain injury, and other developmental differences.

She has worked in early intervention and education for more than 30 years and has held board certification as a behavior analyst since 2004.

Approach

Naturalistic applied behavior analysis

Her interventions are grounded in naturalistic applications of applied behavior analysis and are shaped by the values, beliefs, and goals of each family she works with.

University roles

Leadership at UO

Assistant Research Professor, Associate Director, and faculty member in the College of Education and Psychology network.

Work style

Practical support

She emphasizes coaching, telehealth, and intervention strategies that are realistic for families and educators.

Strength

Bridging research and practice

Her work connects scholarship, training, and community support so research can be used in everyday settings.
