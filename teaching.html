Teaching and mentorship

Preparing the next generation of early intervention professionals.

Dr. Kunze teaches, advises, and supervises across early intervention, early childhood special education, and applied behavior analysis. Her teaching emphasizes practical skills, collaborative problem solving, and family-centered care.

Teaching is closely connected to her research, so students learn approaches that can be used in real educational and clinical settings.
